- On 2026-08-09, P15 was corrected from the earlier erroneous `Short` label to

- The same final audit restored official proceedings DOIs for P04, P13, and
  P14, and the Main Conference Track label for P14. Full before/after history
  is retained in `artifacts/s0/literature_manifest.json`.
- All local PDFs came from legal public arXiv endpoints. On 2026-08-09 all 16
  independently passed existence, non-empty, `%PDF-`, `%%EOF`, byte-size,
  SHA-256, and extracted title/author identity checks. PDFs remain local-only
  and ignored by Git.
